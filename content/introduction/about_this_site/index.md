### What this site will be about

This site is a campaign site, made by Bahman Altamash to help the campaign of fairer fines. The campaign in question - folks for fairer fines - is led by both him and Kareem Aziz. Progress on our survey and petition(s) will be posted on to this site, as well as new research into our campaign.

#### The aim of our campaign

As advocates, we want an overhaul of the current court fine system in the UK. As of now, many feel that court fines are essentially a slap on the wrist for the very rich, and can financially cripple the very poor. We believe that court fines should make the offender retribute to society, but that such fines should not leave them in a state where they are unable to pay for basic necessities, such as food and rent. We believe that court fines, as they stand now, criminalise poverty instead of criminalising the actual offence committed. We believe that, instead of being a set amount for the offence, such as £2,500 for crime XYZ, we believe that fines should be a proportion of one's income, so that the poorest and richest can help the government in their own ways, while still having full stomachs and roofs over their heads. 
We want:
* Fines to be higher to ensure that the rich contribute more to society
* The state to lower fines for misdemeanor offences
* To allow people to pay in monthly installments if they so choose

#### How you can help

As of now, we are currently running a petition for our campaign. You can help by signing it [_here_](https://www.change.org/p/reform-court-fines-to-support-fairness-for-all).

We are also conducting our own research by creating a survey. By completing this survey [_here_](https://forms.gle/9w33eiy8C3aBUWHi8), you will allow us to gain an insight as to how court fines actually impact people's finances.
